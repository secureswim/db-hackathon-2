10 / PARTICIPATION

Rules of the sprint.

Clear requirements help teams focus on engineering, collaboration, and a working demonstration.

Teams consist of 2–4 members.
The hackathon runs for 72 hours from 2–4 October.
Teams must complete all required checkpoints.
Every team must submit a working demonstration.
Every submission must include an architecture diagram.
Databricks technologies should be meaningfully incorporated.
Open-source models and third-party APIs may be used where permitted.
AI coding assistants may be used, but participants must understand their implementation.
Document external datasets, models, APIs, and dependencies.
Teams must explain their architecture and technical decisions.
Substantially copied or plagiarized submissions may be disqualified.
PPI opportunities are based on hackathon evaluation.
